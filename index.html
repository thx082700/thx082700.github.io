Research

GeoOUFormer: Geometry-Anchored Attention for Joint Point Cloud Denoising and Upsampling

Haoxi Tian

Under review at CVPR 2027

I study how to recover object surfaces from noisy or sparse 3D points. When the model deletes spatial cells during generation, sequence-based grouping can change which neighbors other cells consult. GeoOUFormer groups cells by spatial location to keep these relationships stable. On 120 PUNet denoising inputs, mean Chamfer and Hausdorff distances fell by 3.35% and 19.34% versus Serial-OUFormer, without adding parameters; overall upsampling performance remained close to the baseline.

From Fragments to Objects: Diagnosing Object-Set Grounding in 3D Maps

Haoxi Tian

Under review at HOUR, WACV 2027 Workshop

A 3D map may store a chair’s seat and backrest under separate IDs, so retrieving two fragments does not necessarily mean finding two chairs. I built a workflow that uses color and depth images to check which fragments belong together, review uncertain judgments, and decide whether an answer is supported. Across 60 queries in five Replica scenes, the study separates missing candidates, mistaken object identities, confirmation rules, and incomplete localization. Stricter checks can block both wrong and correct answers without fixing the underlying object grouping.
