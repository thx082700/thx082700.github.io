Point cloud restorationUnder review · CVPR 2027

GAAFormerGeometry-Anchored Attention Transformer for Joint Point Cloud Denoising and Upsampling

Developed geometry-anchored decoder attention within a shared octree transformer. On 120 PUNet denoising inputs, mean CD decreased by 3.35% and HD by 19.34% versus the matched serialized-attention backbone, with no additional learned parameters.

3D object groundingUnder review · HOUR at WACV 2027

From Fragments to ObjectsDiagnosing Object-Set Grounding in 3D Maps

Built an evidence-indexed diagnostic workflow over five fixed 3D maps. Across 60 queries and three runs, matched-response controls separated candidate coverage, physical identity, answer confirmation, and object localization.

About

From civil engineering
to 3D perception.

My civil engineering training introduced me to mechanics, modeling, and the assumptions behind a numerical result. In my graduation design, I modeled a prestressed-concrete railway bridge in MIDAS/Civil and analyzed construction stages and moving train loads.

Through point cloud competitions and research, I developed an interest in how a machine interprets three-dimensional geometry. I now want to connect that work with the design and control of robotic systems.

I have also developed a simple garment hanging-system program for my family's factory and completed additional coursework in data structures and algorithms and deep learning.
