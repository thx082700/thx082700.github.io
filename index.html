Research

GAAFormer: Geometry-Anchored Attention Transformer for Joint Point Cloud Denoising and Upsampling

Haoxi Tian

Under review at CVPR 2027

Fixed spatial attention windows stabilize neighborhoods as octree branches change. On 120 PUNet denoising inputs, mean Chamfer distance decreased by 3.35% and Hausdorff distance by 19.34% versus Serial-OUFormer, with no added learned parameters.

[Project][Results]

From Fragments to Objects: Diagnosing Object-Set Grounding in 3D Maps

Haoxi Tian

Under review at HOUR, WACV 2027 Workshop

A diagnostic workflow separates missing candidates, object identity, answer confirmation, and localization. Evaluated on 60 count queries across five Replica scenes in three runs, with matched-response controls to isolate confirmation effects.
